## 3. What Hullabye does

Hullabye lets you log a child's naps, night sleep and wake times, and gives **estimated** nap and bedtime times based on the child's age and the day's sleep so far.

**Free version** includes: one child, today's predictions, the last 3 days of history, with data stored on your device only. Hullabye doesn't sync it, except a log someone shares with you, which syncs through iCloud.

**Hullabye Premium** adds: iCloud sync across your devices, sharing with a partner or carer, multiple children, full history and the weekly trend, and reminders.

**Hullabye is a sleep-tracking and planning tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.**

- Nap and bedtime predictions are **estimates** based on general patterns for a child's age and the records you enter. Every child is different, and predictions may not suit your child.
- Hullabye is **not a substitute** for advice from your **health visitor, GP, midwife, paediatrician or another qualified health professional**. Always seek their advice about your child's health, development, feeding or sleep, and don't ignore or delay getting professional advice because of anything in the app.
- Hullabye is **not a baby monitor** and doesn't monitor breathing, movement or wellbeing.
- Always follow safer sleep guidance from your national health service. In the UK, that includes the NHS (<https://www.nhs.uk/baby/caring-for-a-newborn/reduce-the-risk-of-sudden-infant-death-syndrome/>) and The Lullaby Trust (<https://www.lullabytrust.org.uk/>).
