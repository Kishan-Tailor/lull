# Hullabye: Baby Sleep – Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 27 November 2026
**App:** Hullabye: Baby Sleep (iOS) ("Hullabye", "the app")
**Developer:** Kishan Tailor, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom ("I", "me", "my")
**Contact:** lullapp@icloud.com

---

## The short version

- **I don't collect the information you enter.** Hullabye has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and I don't run any servers. I can't see anything you record in the app.
- **Your child's information stays with you.** It's stored on your iPhone (and, like other app data, may be included in your device backups, such as iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer). If you subscribe to Hullabye Premium, it's also synced through **your own private iCloud account**, which Apple runs and I can't access.
- **You decide who else sees it.** If you invite a partner or carer, they can see the shared records through their own iCloud account. You can manage or stop sharing in the app while Premium is active, and you can end all sharing at any time with Delete All Data.
- **You can delete it whenever you like**, in the app or in your iCloud settings.

The rest of this policy explains the details.

---

## 1. Who this policy applies to

Hullabye is designed for **parents and carers** to keep track of a baby's or young child's sleep. It is not meant to be used by children.

This policy is written with UK data protection law in mind, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Hullabye is available worldwide. If you live outside the UK, you may have additional rights under your local law (for example, the EU GDPR if you're in the European Union), and nothing in this policy takes those rights away.

## 2. Information you enter in the app

To work, Hullabye lets you enter the following. It is stored on your device, and in your private iCloud if you use Premium sync:

| Information | Why the app uses it |
|---|---|
| Your child's name (or a nickname) | To label their profile, especially if you track more than one child |
| Your child's date of birth | To work out their age, which the app uses to predict naps and bedtime |
| Sleep records: morning wake times, naps and night sleep (start and end times) | To show history and the weekly trend and to make predictions |
| App settings, such as reminder times and preferences | To make the app work the way you've set it up |

**Tip:** you don't have to use your child's real name. A nickname or initial works just as well.

All the calculations, including nap and bedtime predictions, happen **on your device**.

## 3. What I collect from the app: nothing

I, the developer, do **not** collect, receive, store, sell or share any of the information you enter in Hullabye. Specifically:

- **No accounts or sign-in.** You never give me your name, email address or phone number to use the app.
- **No servers.** I don't operate any servers or databases that your data passes through.
- **No analytics or tracking.** The app doesn't contain analytics tools, tracking pixels, or device fingerprinting, and it doesn't track you across other apps or websites.
- **No advertising.** There are no ads and no advertising SDKs.
- **No third-party SDKs.** Hullabye is built only with Apple's own frameworks.

Because of this, I have no way to view, recover or hand over the information you've entered, even if you ask me to.

## 4. Where your data is stored: your device, your backups and iCloud

**On your device.** Hullabye stores your records on your iPhone. Like other app data, they may be included in your iPhone's backups (iCloud Backup, or a backup to your computer) if you have backups turned on. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by me.

**On the free version**, Hullabye itself doesn't sync your data anywhere, except a log someone shares with you, which syncs through iCloud (see section 5).

**With Hullabye Premium**, you can sync your data across your Apple devices. Hullabye does this using **Apple's CloudKit** service, which stores your data in the **private database of your own iCloud account**.

- Your data is held by **Apple** under your iCloud account and Apple's terms, not by me. I can't access your private iCloud database.
- Apple encrypts iCloud data in transit and when it is stored. The protections available can vary by country and by your iCloud settings. You can read more in Apple's iCloud security overview: <https://support.apple.com/en-gb/102651>
- Apple may store iCloud data on servers outside your country. Apple's handling of your data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>
- Syncing uses a small amount of your iCloud storage.
- If you're not signed in to iCloud, or you've turned iCloud off for Hullabye, your data stays on your device (and in any device backups).

## 5. Sharing with a partner or carer (Hullabye Premium)

Premium lets you invite another person, such as a partner, grandparent or nanny, to view and add to a child's sleep records. This uses Apple's **CloudKit sharing** feature.

- **You choose who to invite**, and you send the invitation yourself from the child's Settings screen (**Share with Partner**), using Apple's standard sharing options (for example Messages or email).
- **You choose their access:** you can give each person **read-only** access or let them add and edit entries.
- The person you invite needs the Hullabye app and an iCloud account, but **doesn't need their own Premium subscription**. Your subscription covers them, and the shared log syncs to them through iCloud even on the free version.
- They access the shared records **through their own iCloud account**. While your Premium is active, they can view the shared child's records and, if you've allowed it, add or edit entries.
- **If your Premium ends**, existing shares carry on in **read-only** mode. The people you've invited can still view the records shared up to then, but can't add or edit them. New entries you make while Premium is off stay on your device and are shared again when you renew. Your data is kept.
- Apple shows you, as the owner of the share, the names or Apple ID details of the people taking part, and shows them yours. I don't receive any of this.
- **Stopping sharing:** while your Premium is active, you can stop sharing or remove a person from the child's Settings screen (**Manage Sharing**). You can also end all sharing at any time, with or without Premium, by using **Delete All Data** (**Settings > Your data**). Someone who has been shared with can leave at any time from **Sharing Options > Leave**. Once removed, people no longer receive updates or have access through iCloud. However, they may already have seen the information, and a copy of records shared before you removed them may remain on their device until the app removes it or they delete the app.
- Please only invite people you trust, and only share information about a child you are responsible for.

## 6. Subscriptions and payments

Hullabye Premium subscriptions are bought through **Apple's App Store** using In-App Purchase.

- Apple processes your payment. I never see your card details, name, address or Apple ID.
- The app checks whether you have an active subscription using Apple's StoreKit, on your device.
- Apple gives developers sales and subscription reports in App Store Connect. These don't tell me who you are or link to anything you've entered in Hullabye.

Apple's handling of purchase information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

## 7. Reminders and notifications

If you turn on reminders (a Premium feature), Hullabye schedules **local notifications** on your device. They are created and delivered by your iPhone itself. No push notification server is used, and nothing is sent to me. You can turn notifications off at any time in the app or in **Settings > Notifications > Hullabye**.

## 8. Crash reports and diagnostics shared by Apple

Hullabye itself doesn't collect crash reports or usage data. However, if you have chosen in iOS to **share analytics with app developers**, Apple may give me:

- **crash logs**, which include technical details such as your device model, iOS version and where in the app the crash happened;
- **aggregated usage statistics**, such as how many people opened the app on a given day.

I receive these through App Store Connect. They don't include your name, your Apple ID or anything you've entered in Hullabye. I use them only to find and fix bugs. My lawful basis is my legitimate interest in keeping the app working, and I delete crash logs within 12 months. You can turn this sharing off at any time in **Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements**.

## 9. If you contact me

If you email me for support, I'll receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. I use this only to reply to you and to help with your question. My lawful basis for this is my legitimate interest in providing support to people who use my app.

- Emails are stored with my email provider, Apple (iCloud Mail), who acts as my processor and may store data outside the UK. Where that happens, it is protected by appropriate safeguards required by UK data protection law (such as an adequacy decision, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses).
- I keep support emails for no longer than I need to (normally no more than 24 months), and I'll delete them sooner if you ask.
- I don't add you to mailing lists.

**Please don't send me sensitive details about your child** (such as health information) by email. I don't need them to help you.

## 10. Children's data

Hullabye is used by parents and carers to record information **about** their child. I take the sensitivity of this seriously, which is why the app is designed so that this information never comes to me at all:

- It stays on your device (and your own backups) and, if you choose, in your own private iCloud.
- It is never used for advertising, profiling, marketing or any purpose other than showing you your child's sleep records and predictions.
- It is never sold or shared by me with anyone.
- You stay in control of it and can delete it at any time.

Hullabye is not directed at children and children should not use it themselves. Only enter information about a child you are a parent or carer of.

## 11. My role under data protection law

The information you enter in Hullabye is processed on your device and stored in your own iCloud account. I don't receive, store or have access to it, and I don't decide what you record or who you share it with. I therefore don't consider myself a controller or processor of that information. When you record information about your own family, the "household" exemption in UK GDPR is likely to apply to you. Apple is responsible for iCloud, as set out in its own privacy policy.

I act as a **controller** only for:

- support emails you send me (section 9); and
- crash reports and statistics that Apple shares with me (section 8).

## 12. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept for as long as you keep it in the app. You can delete it at any time:

- **In the app:** you can delete individual sleep entries or a child's profile, or use **Delete All Data** (**Settings > Your data > Delete All Data**) to erase everything, including your Hullabye data in iCloud, and end all sharing. While Premium is active, these deletions also apply to your iCloud copy and to your other devices that sync with it. **If your Premium has ended**, deleting a child removes it from that iPhone only. People you shared it with keep their copy until you renew or use Delete All Data.
- **In iCloud settings:** on your iPhone, go to **Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage** (the exact wording varies between iOS versions), choose **Hullabye**, and select **Delete Data**. This removes Hullabye's data from your iCloud account.
- **Deleting the app** removes the data stored on that device. If you've used iCloud sync, a copy will remain in your iCloud account until you delete it as described above.
- **Backups:** copies may remain in older iCloud or computer backups until those backups are deleted or replaced.
- **Shared records:** if, while Premium is active, you delete a child you've shared or stop sharing, the people you invited lose access to those records through iCloud (see section 5 about copies already on their device). Delete All Data ends all sharing at any time. If someone has shared a child with you, you can leave the share from **Sharing Options > Leave**, but only the owner can delete the original records.

Because I don't hold your data, I can't delete it for you, but I'm happy to help you with the steps.

## 13. Your rights

Under UK data protection law (and similar laws elsewhere), you have rights including the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability.

- **Information in the app:** I don't hold this, so you can exercise these rights directly by viewing, editing or deleting your data in the app or in iCloud. For data held by Apple, you can also contact Apple at <https://privacy.apple.com/>.
- **Support emails and crash reports:** contact me at lullapp@icloud.com and I'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how your data has been handled, you can complain to the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)**, the UK's data protection regulator: <https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/> or 0303 123 1113. If you live outside the UK, you can also contact your local data protection authority. I'd appreciate the chance to sort out your concern first, so please get in touch.

## 14. Security

Hullabye relies on the security built into iOS and iCloud. Data on your iPhone is protected by iOS data protection when your device is locked with a passcode, Face ID or Touch ID. To keep your child's information safe, use a device passcode, keep iOS up to date, and protect your Apple ID with two-factor authentication.

## 15. Changes to this policy

If I change how Hullabye handles information, I'll update this policy and change the effective date at the top. If a change is significant (for example, if the app ever started collecting any data), I'll make it clear in the app or in the App Store release notes before the change takes effect.
