# Lull: Baby Sleep – Support

Need help? Email **lullapp@icloud.com**. I'm an independent developer and I aim to reply within a few working days.

When you write, it helps to include your iPhone model, iOS version and Lull version (shown in **Settings > About**). **Please don't send health details about your child.** I don't need them to help you.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I add a Lull widget?
- **Home Screen:** touch and hold the Home Screen, tap **Edit > Add Widget**, choose **Lull**, then pick the small or medium widget. It shows the next nap or bedtime and has a **Start nap** button.
- **Lock Screen:** touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap **Customize > Lock Screen**, tap the widget area and choose **Lull**. The rectangular widget has a **Start nap** button (it becomes **Start bedtime** in the evening); the circular one shows the next time.
- **While your baby sleeps:** a Live Activity shows the timer on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Tap **Woke up** to end the sleep. If it doesn't appear, check **Settings > Lull > Live Activities** is on.
- Widgets are free. Add a child in the app first. If a child was shared with you as view-only, the widget shows the times without the buttons.

### What happens if my Premium ends?
You keep all your data. You can still see every child you've added, but you can't add more, and history shows the last 3 days. Sync between your devices, the weekly trend and reminders stop. Shared children become read-only for partners. Everything comes back when you renew.

### How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Go to **Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions** on your iPhone and choose Lull. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of your trial or current period to avoid being charged for the next one. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

### How do I get a refund?
Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at <https://reportaproblem.apple.com>.

### I've got a new phone and Premium isn't showing.
Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID, then go to **Settings > Lull Premium > Restore Purchases** in Lull.

### My data isn't syncing between devices.
Sync is a Premium feature and uses your own iCloud. Check that:
1. you're signed in to iCloud on each device with the same Apple ID;
2. iCloud is turned on for Lull (Settings > [your name] > iCloud);
3. you have some free iCloud storage and an internet connection.

Sync can take a few minutes.

### How does sharing with a partner work?
With Premium, open your child's **Settings** screen and choose **Share with Partner** to send an invitation. You can give each person read-only or edit access. Your partner needs the Lull app and an iCloud account, but **not** their own subscription. The shared log syncs to them through iCloud. On a new install, it can take a few moments to appear.

- **Managing sharing:** while Premium is active, use **Manage Sharing** on the child's Settings screen to change access, remove someone or stop sharing.
- **Leaving a share:** if someone shared a child with you, use **Sharing Options > Leave**.
- **If your Premium ends:** partners can still view what was shared up to then, but nobody can add or edit. New entries you make stay on your phone and are shared again when you renew. You can't manage sharing until you renew, but **Settings > Your data > Delete All Data** ends all sharing at any time.

### How do I delete my data?
- In Lull: delete an entry, delete a child, or use **Settings > Your data > Delete All Data**, which also deletes your Lull data from iCloud and ends all sharing. With Premium active, deletions sync to your iCloud and devices. If Premium has ended, deleting a child removes it from that iPhone only, and partners keep their copy until you renew or use Delete All Data.
- In iCloud: **Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage > Lull > Delete Data**. The wording varies by iOS version.
- Older device backups may still contain copies until they're replaced.

I can't see or delete your data myself, because Lull never sends it to me. See the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy-policy/' | relative_url }}).

### Are the predictions medical advice?
No. Predictions are estimates based on your baby's age and today's sleep. Always talk to your health visitor, GP or other health professional about your baby's health and sleep. In an emergency, call 999 or your local emergency number.
